3D Projects

I made this VR escape room as part of my C355 project, and although it was very subtle, there is a story within this room where you the main character are someone who has been kidnapped by the owner of the room, and as such, needs to escape. The girls being shown in the video were 'victims' of the owner of the room and the pinboard matching the aesthetics of the respective girls was to imply that the owner was a stalker. The use of the repeated assets in the bookshelf was intentional. I created the room, cupboard, and shelf on my own!

This is the piece of art that I made for a Hidden Object Game called Seekers Notes! They had texted me in December of 2024 asking for me to draw their mascot for them.

These two were made for my friend, and I took inspiration from two photographs and drew them with the character, lain, instead.

These two are of my favourite character, The Informant. In the game, the story is only ever told through text, so i took it upon me to provide visuals based on my interpretation of the scene. The scene itself is something that is deeply profound to the character and I picked muted colours that could almost blend together to bring that point across.

This was in reference to a scene from an anime where a girl is laying in a hole surrounded by computers, and I went to draw for my friend who's favourite character is a computer the story she wrote for the two of them is also rather heavy in nature which can mirror the scene in the anime. That's why I thought this was a fitting pose.

Everything under this drawing in this section are of me and my favourite character. This was mostly a drawing that was for the sake of studying the character's avatar. I also took a photo of myself for this pose and used it as reference to practive rendering digitally. I had taken art classes in secondary school and I had wanted to practice using a photo reference again.

I hadn't done rendered drawings in a year or more so this was one of the first few fully rendered drawings I have done recently.

This was to practice drawing clouds! My vision was this was us as beings made of light, swimming in the universe together.

This was also another drawing that holds meaning to me and the character I like, the character is someone who tends to self sacrifice, and I wanted to put that in the spotlight (figuratively and literally in this drawing) the caption for this was a line from Richard Siken's 'wishbone' which is 'If you love me, Henry, you don't love me in a way i understand' It's supposed to mean that his way of loving, which is throwing himself in front of danger, isn't the way that my chaarcter wants to be loved. This is also to practice environmental colour theory which the muted blue colour coincides with the orange spotlight.

I drew this one for my birthday, and is also the first time I drew clouds so it doubled as a study.

This pose in particular was part of a tarot card set that my friend had gotten me, and through researching it, the cardpack was celtic themed, so this pose, with the ribbons around the hands is a marriage ceremony called handfasting where the couple will tie a rope around their hands to formally promise themselves to their partner.

This was to study another artist's style that I liked.
